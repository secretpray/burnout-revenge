# Classic Crash Launch

Enable **Settings > Mods > Classic Crash Launch**, then restart the game.
The option is off by default. It applies to solo World Tour Crash with
**Online Play disabled**. Other modes keep their original launch.

Tap and release the launch button (Xbox A, PlayStation cross, or its keyboard
binding) once to begin. The meter rises on its own. Tap again to set speed,
release, then tap again to set control as it descends. Holding the first press
can stop the meter at about one third and trigger a weak automatic start;
this follows the original PS2/Xbox eligible-button rules, rather than a lost
controller signal. The upper green zone sets a fast launch; combining it with the lower
green zone gives Power Boost. The extreme red ends can produce Engine Blown
or Stalled. The Stall window is very narrow and depends on the simulation
frame; a missed press can give Slow Start instead. Waiting without pressing
also finishes automatically after the original meter cycles.

The rules use the original PS2/Xbox nonlinear movement, eligible-button
history, per-car thresholds, speed and steering results, engine controls and
failure deadlines. Time and traffic run while you choose the launch; finishing
the meter does not reset the world. Stall and Blown have the original five-second
failure deadline.

The curved bar and flames use retained artwork from your installed Xbox 360
game. Outcome text and animations use its localized HUD. Fonts, explosion and
camera effects can differ from PS2/Xbox. Blown currently uses the Xbox 360 crash,
explosion and upward impulse; the original body-part damage and dedicated
camera sequence are not reproduced. Sound parameters were checked against the
original algorithm, but audio has not been verified by listening.

The current event's Replay retains the launch timeline and driving controls
in memory, including replaying from the start. Small physics differences can
remain. Saved or reloaded Burnout Clips do not store this additional timeline
and can play differently. Rewind history starts after the launch meter finishes.
